Sample review brief — Hoppy Apps

Disclaimer: this brief was prepared from public Shopify App Store reviews as a demonstration of the service format. Hoppy Apps did not commission or endorse it. It is a sample, not an allegation of unresolved defects; reviews are customer reports and should be verified against support tickets and telemetry. Also published as a public gist.

Executive signal

Highest-priority owned-app risk: access/editor failure in Hoppy PopUps. Three 1-star reviews in April–May 2026 report that the app would not open, showed a blank screen, or simply did not work. Two reports came from users in their first 10 hours, so this may hit activation before value is reached.

Owned app: Hoppy PopUps

Public listing: apps.shopify.com/suppr-email-collection-bar

Prioritized signals — Hoppy PopUps
PrioritySignalEvidenceSuggested next action
P0 App/editor inaccessible May 26: blank screen and inability to edit after prior work; May 7: “no me deja entrar a la app”; Apr 8: “No funciona” Segment startup errors by browser/locale/store; reproduce against the affected activation path; add a recoverable error state instead of blank UI.
P1 Review-request timing creates mistrust Feb 23 reviewer says the app asked for a review before they could try it Trigger review requests only after a measurable success event, not during initial activation.

Pattern: 4 of 5 one-star reviews shown in the public 1-star filter describe either inability to use the product or frustration before value. The fastest win is activation reliability and instrumentation, not another popup template.

Source: Hoppy PopUps 1-star reviews

Competitor watch: Wheelify Spin Wheel Discount

Public listing: apps.shopify.com/spin-wheel

Competitor signals — Wheelify
SignalRecent evidenceProduct implication for Hoppy
Pricing surprise May 18 and Feb 20, 2026 reviews complain about usage fees/basic features after setup Keep Hoppy PopUps’ free-plan boundaries explicit before configuration and show estimated usage before any charge.
Broken core output Historical reviews report invalid generated discount codes and inaccessible subscriber exports Make “verified code at checkout” and export reliability visible trust points in onboarding/marketing if telemetry supports the claim.

Source: Wheelify 1-star reviews

Suggested one-week experiment

Instrument the Hoppy PopUps editor boot sequence and create a support alert when a new user sees two consecutive boot failures. Compare activation-to-first-published-popup before and after adding a recovery state. This directly tests the clearest recent low-star pattern.
