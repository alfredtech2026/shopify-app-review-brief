Who this is for

  • ICP: independent Shopify app teams managing 3+ active apps with public App Store reviews and a support/product owner who can act on feedback.
  • Trigger: a new 1–3-star review appears on one of your apps or on a named competitor.

What you get

Within one business day, new low-star reviews across your portfolio and up to five named competitors become one prioritized brief covering:

  • incident risk (is the purchase path or activation at stake?),
  • repeated friction patterns across apps and time,
  • pricing confusion signals,
  • feature requests worth tracking,
  • a suggested product/support action for each item, with source links to every review cited.

Every brief is human-checked. Reviews are customer reports, not verified defects — the brief says so explicitly and links each claim to its public source so your team can verify against support tickets and telemetry.

Transparent boundaries

What this service is not:

  • It uses public Shopify App Store data only — no private merchant data, no privileged API access.
  • It makes no claim of exhaustive coverage: a brief covers the reviews found on public listing pages during weekday monitoring.
  • It makes no guarantee of revenue impact and provides no legal or compliance advice.
  • This is a new service in validation. There are no customers yet and no testimonials — the sample briefs above are the proof of work.

How the opt-in inquiry works

  1. You open the prefilled GitHub issue (a free GitHub account is required to post).
  2. We reply on the issue to confirm scope — your apps, competitors, and where to send briefs.
  3. Monitoring starts; the first brief arrives within one business day of the first qualifying review (or as a baseline portfolio brief).
  4. Only after you say the first brief was useful do we invoice the $49 pilot fee.
