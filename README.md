# Shopify App Review Brief

Public landing page and sample briefs for a concierge service that turns new 1–3-star Shopify App Store reviews into one human-checked, prioritized product/support brief within one business day.

- **Who:** independent Shopify app teams managing 3+ active apps
- **Pilot:** 14 days, up to 10 owned apps and 5 competitors
- **Price:** $49, charged only after the buyer approves the first useful brief
- **Acquisition:** opt-in only; no cold email or cold DMs
